US Sites · FY26 (Oct–Jun) & Summer 2026 (Jun–Aug)

PeacePlayers US Data Report

Program, participant, and outcome data across five US PeacePlayers sites — Baltimore, Brooklyn, Chicago, Detroit, and LA. Annual and Summer programs are shown as two separate programs, not compared to one another.

Reading this page. Annual Program runs roughly twice a week, October through June. Summer Program runs up to five days a week, June through August, at three of the five sites. Where a site has no data for a section, that's stated directly rather than left blank. International sites (Middle East, Northern Ireland, South Africa) are intentionally excluded from this page.
